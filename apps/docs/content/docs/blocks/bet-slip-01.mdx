## Features

- **Singles and accumulator** — a stake per selection, or one stake across every leg at the combined odds. The accumulator tab is disabled until there are two selections.
- **Live odds** — a stand-in feed moves one price every few seconds. A moved price shows its direction, and the slip asks the punter to accept the new odds before it can be placed, as regulated sportsbooks must.
- **Responsible-gambling limit** — the slip knows today's remaining limit. A stake over it blocks the bet and says how much is left, and that check runs before the odds check, so accepting a new price is never a way past a limit.
- **Money in cents** — stakes and returns are integer minor units, rounded down per selection, so a long slip never totals €30.000000004. Every figure is `tabular-nums`.
- **Placed state** — confirmation with the stake and potential return, and a fresh slip.

The arithmetic lives in `bet-slip-math.ts`, separate from the UI, and `use-odds-feed.ts` is the one file to replace with your price feed's subscription.
