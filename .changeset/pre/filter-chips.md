Add `FilterChips`: pill-shaped choices that filter one list in place

`FilterChips` with `FilterChip` (`@keystoneui/react/filter-chips`) is a single-choice row on a radio group: at most one chip is selected, the selected chip cannot be switched off, and a chip can show a result count. An unselected chip with a count of 0 is disabled unless `disableEmpty={false}`, and `formatCount` prints every count.
